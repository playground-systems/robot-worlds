# Asset sources for generated robot worlds

Checked 2026-10-30 against publisher documentation, public source files and license texts. No simulation, conversion or large asset download was performed. Recommendations below are design judgments; a catalog license is not evidence that an individual mesh is physically valid.

**Use a small licensed asset registry alongside generation:** original parametric factory fixtures, CC0 surface materials, selected RoboCasa articulated objects, and optional NVIDIA SimReady factory models. Keep arbitrary generated layouts and custom objects; the registry supplies reusable detail and interaction definitions rather than limiting what users can describe.

## What RobotRun Forge publicly reveals

This is [Robot Run by Bronti Labs](https://www.robotrun.ai/forge.html), not another project with a similar name. Its Forge client presents prompt-based creation, comparison and adoption of objects. The public [gallery snapshot](https://www.robotrun.ai/forge-gallery-objects.js?v=1), imported by [forge.js](https://www.robotrun.ai/forge.js?v=159), contains 24 objects with model-provider labels, dimensions, palettes, component counts and, for articulated examples, joint counts. For example:

| Gallery object | Declared provider | Declared structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `microwave2` | `openai:gpt-6.1-sol` | 2 components, 1 joint |
| `make a microwave with an openable door` | `gemini:gemini-3.8-flash` | 1 component, 1 joint |
| `tower of hanoi` | `openrouter:moonshotai/kimi-k3` | 6 components |

The snapshot also names Anthropic and another Gemini model. **This supports an interpretation that Forge's gallery contains model-generated objects. It does not identify the server-side geometry method, training sources, possible retrieved assets or validation pipeline.** The public [Build client](https://www.robotrun.ai/build-page.js?v=107) defines the mine/basics/foam/cutlery/home/grocery/tools/puzzle categories and consumes a server catalog; those category names do not establish that catalog's original source. Built-in catalog provenance remains unknown.

The inspected [terms](https://www.robotrun.ai/terms.html), dated 21 October 2026, discuss ownership of submitted content and recordings but do not establish an open redistribution license for the gallery meshes. Do not copy the gallery into our asset library on the strength of public visibility. Research inspected only public HTML/JS/terms: no account, mutation, backend inventory or mesh download. ## Recommended sources

| Need | Practical choice | Verified terms and fit | Remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Factory structure: benches, racks, bins, pallets, fences, conveyor housings | Original dimensioned procedural assemblies; reuse selected robosuite object-generation code where useful | [robosuite's object API](https://robosuite.ai/docs/modules/objects.html) supports primitive/composite MJCF objects and articulated composite bodies; its [software license](https://github.com/ARISE-Initiative/robosuite/blob/master/LICENSE) is MIT with retained notices. This is useful construction code, not a complete photoreal factory catalog. | Author fixture families with separate visual, collision, joint and affordance definitions. Audit any copied third-party mesh or texture separately. |
| Doors, drawers, appliances, containers and manipulation props | Curated RoboCasa subset | The [publisher's repository](https://github.com/robocasa/robocasa#license) states code MIT and assets/datasets CC BY 4.0; its [asset repository](https://huggingface.co/datasets/robocasa/robocasa-assets) also declares CC BY 4.0. [Fixtures](https://robocasa.ai/docs/build/html/assets/fixtures.html) include interactable microwaves, ovens, dishwashers and fridges; [objects](https://robocasa.ai/docs/build/html/assets/objects.html) come from Objaverse, Lightwheel and Luma generation. Best fit is reusable interaction assets, especially beyond factories. | Pin a release and selected IDs, retain creator/source attribution and modifications, inspect included source terms, then convert/check each asset. Household coverage does not supply industrial process machinery. |
| Concrete, metal, wall surfaces, decals and lighting | Small self-hosted Poly Haven / ambientCG CC0 material set | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) allows commercial modification and redistribution of its downloadable HDRIs, textures and models under CC0; its website example renders/logos are excluded. [ambientCG](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) applies CC0 to downloadable assets and material preview renders. | Start with bounded 1K/2K maps. Preserve real-world scale and color-space conventions. PBR maps improve appearance; they do not measure mass, friction or reflectance. |
| Detailed factory/warehouse fixtures | Optional, individually pinned NVIDIA SimReady packages | [SimReady Central](https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/simready/latest/simready-central.html) has package-specific licensing. A concrete [NVIDIA workbench manifest](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/simready-assets/blob/0504fadeace167df506e51a22fc8b76b6940b8cb/furniture_workbench_standard_e01/com.nvidia.simready.packaging.json) explicitly declares CC BY 4.0 and includes the [full license](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/simready-assets/blob/0504fadeace167df506e51a22fc8b76b6940b8cb/furniture_workbench_standard_e01/.metadata/LICENSE.txt). This is the strongest factory-specific candidate checked. | USD/material conversion, dependency resolution, collider rebuilding, size reduction and native Mac physics/visual checks remain untested. Do not infer the same license for every SimReady pack. |

CC BY 4.0 permits commercial sharing and adaptation with appropriate credit, a license link and change indications; original asset terms remain distinct from our MIT code. See the [license's own summary](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en).

A useful first surface palette is [Hangar Concrete Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/hangar_concrete_floor), [Factory Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/factory_wall), and [Metal Plates 006](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=MetalPlates006). [Machine Shop 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/machine_shop_02) is an optional industrial HDRI, not navigable scene geometry. Generate our own thumbnails for Poly Haven materials. Its [live API terms](https://github.com/Poly-Haven/Public-API/blob/master/ToS.md) require identifiable requests and visible credit; those API conditions are separate from self-hosted downloaded CC0 files.

For the checked NVIDIA workbench, revision `0504fadeace167df506e51a22fc8b76b6940b8cb` and license SHA-256 `9ba9550ad48438d0836ddab3da480b3b69ffa0aac7b7878b5a0039e7ab429411` are recorded. Only its small manifest/license/bill of materials were fetched; the roughly 441 MB geometry package was not inspected. The [classic pack catalog](https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/usd/latest/usd_content_samples/downloadable_packs.html) offers industrial and warehouse coverage, but its “free to use” wording is insufficient evidence for bundling each archive. Avoid downloading whole multi-gigabyte catalogs by default.

## Sources to keep out of the default bundle

| Source | Finding and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| PartNet-Mobility / SAPIEN | The [official dataset terms](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sapien-sim/PartNetMobility) limit use to noncommercial research/education and incorporate ShapeNet terms. SAPIEN's software availability does not remove those dataset restrictions. Keep as a separate user-supplied research integration if needed. |
| BEHAVIOR / OmniGibson | The [first-party data agreement in setup.sh](https://github.com/StanfordVL/BEHAVIOR-1K/blob/main/setup.sh) limits the Data Bundle to noncommercial academic use within OmniGibson, prohibits extraction and redistribution, and distinguishes it from MIT software. The [asset-source page](https://behavior.stanford.edu/behavior_components/asset_sources.html) describes purchased TurboSquid assets. This is not a source for exporting meshes into our runtime. |
| Objaverse | The [Objaverse-XL repository](https://github.com/allenai/objaverse-xl#license) distinguishes its database license from individual object licenses. Use only a curated per-object allowlist with source/creator/license evidence; the dataset name is not one blanket commercial redistribution grant. Retrieval also does not provide collision, joints or calibrated physical properties. |
| RoboTwin / RoboVerse | RoboTwin's [official object documentation](https://robotwin-platform.github.io/doc/objects/index.html) describes a mix of generated meshes, Objaverse objects and 44 PartNet-Mobility instances. Its [MIT-tagged download repository](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TianxingChen/RoboTwin2.0) does not resolve that component-level distinction for our bundle. [RoboVerse's notices](https://github.com/RoboVerseOrg/RoboVerse/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) explicitly send asset consumers back to original terms. Both are useful integration references; neither should be treated as a uniformly licensed asset grab bag. |

## A Mac-compatible ingestion path

[OpenUSD supports macOS](https://github.com/PixarAnimationStudios/OpenUSD/blob/release/README.md); [Isaac Sim's local requirements](https://docs.isaacsim.omniverse.nvidia.com/latest/installation/requirements.html) list supported Linux/Windows and NVIDIA hardware. Use native USD inspection or conversion tooling rather than making Isaac Sim a Mac onboarding dependency. OpenUSD's software license is independent of a USD asset's content license.

The proposed pipeline is source asset → pinned local package → normalized visuals/materials → explicit collision/joint/affordance definitions → our validation. [MuJoCo's mesh format](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/latest/XMLreference.html#asset-mesh) accepts OBJ, binary STL and MSH, not arbitrary USD/MDL scenes. Mesh collision is a convex hull; a one-piece visual bin or rack can lose its openings physically. Keep hollow/concave fixtures as deliberate components or collision proxies. Preserve joint axes, limits, units, transforms and persistent part identity when importing articulated objects.

Each registry entry should record source URL/revision, creator, license/notice, original and converted hashes, dimensions, visual format, collision method, joint/affordance definitions and tests actually passed. A generated asset can enter the same registry after validation. Unverified materials and physics stay labeled as estimates. This lets us improve fidelity and reuse proven interactions while retaining text-first arbitrary scene generation.

No candidate geometry was loaded into Robot Worlds during this research. The remaining decision is which small subset to import and validate first; the recommendation is factory primitives plus three CC0 surfaces, one explicitly licensed detailed workbench, and one RoboCasa articulated fixture.
