## Homebody: the user's visual target

Astra directly inspected [Homebody's interactive reconstruction](https://tml.stanford.edu/homebody/) in overview, kitchen and top-down views, plus its matched refrigerator-wall and island simulation/real images. The reconstruction preserves complete cabinet runs, appliance proportions, island/stool relationships, material identity, thin handles, sinks, outlets and localized countertop objects. The interactive view uses simpler lighting than the displayed Isaac images, but remains coherent at working height. This is the visual bar: complete spaces, readable materials and relevant detail, not merely high object counts.

Authored acceptance requires both an interior robot-height view and an overview; believable fixture proportions; preserved texture scale and surface response; legible handles/cavities; task props placed in real work areas; and lighting that reveals material and shape without flattening them. RoboCasa remains selected pending its actual render. The current Molmo classic-renderer still is below this bar. Factory v3 needs functional equipment diversity, not additional duplicate cartons.

Homebody is a reference, not an approved source switch. Engineering's shipped-source audit finds four free props and no drawer hinges/slides. The website's illustrative rollout and real-robot drawer demonstrations do not establish an articulated imported kitchen. No reference image or website mesh was copied into our scene during this review.
